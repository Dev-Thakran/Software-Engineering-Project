Our Story

A quiet devotion
to hospitality.

La Extravaganza opened its doors in 2014 as a single address with a single idea — that hospitality is less about grandeur and more about noticing.

Four floors rise above the harbour. Each was imagined as a distinct chapter — the Garden, the Classic, the Executive, and at the summit, the Penthouse. Every floor has its own character, its own light, its own pace.

Our team numbers fifty-two. Some have been with us since the beginning. Others are newer — but all share one conviction: the finest stays are made of small, well-timed gestures.

We are not the largest hotel in the city. We do not intend to be. We are simply a place that has been considered, from the weight of the door to the warmth of the bread. We hope it shows.
