Terms & Privacy

How booking, cancelling and changing a stay works, and what we do with your details. Last updated 28 October 2026.

Please note: La Extravaganza is a student software project built for the School of Digital Technologies Tech Expo. It is not a real hotel - no real payments are taken and no stays are provided. These terms describe how the demo works.

Booking & payment

  • Room prices are shown per night in New Zealand dollars.
  • A flat $30 taxes & fees is added once per booking. The full total is always shown before you pay.
  • Your booking is confirmed once payment goes through, and you'll see your booking reference straight away. It's also saved in My Stays.
  • You need an account to book, and you must be the person named on the booking or booking on their behalf.

Rates

Every room can be booked at one of two rates. You choose on the booking form.

  • Flexible: the standard nightly price. Free cancellation and date changes until 23:59 the day before you arrive.
  • Advance purchase: 10% off the room price, paid in full when you book. It is non-refundable and can't be cancelled or changed online. If something comes up, call reception on +64 9 123 4567 and we'll do what we can.
  • A promo code can be used with either rate. On Advance purchase it comes off the already-reduced price.

Cancellation

  • Free cancellation until 23:59 (New Zealand time) the day before you arrive. Cancel any time before then from My Stays. This applies to the Flexible rate; Advance purchase bookings are non-refundable.
  • On the day of arrival, cancellations and changes need the front desk: call us on +64 9 123 4567.
  • If you don't arrive and haven't cancelled (a no-show), the stay is charged in full.

Changing your dates

  • You can move an upcoming stay to new dates from My Stays, up to the same cut-off as cancelling: 23:59 the day before arrival. Advance purchase bookings can only be changed by reception.
  • The room must be free for every night of the new dates. Your stay is re-priced at the room's current nightly rate, and any promo code you used still applies.
  • If the new stay costs more, the difference is paid at check-in. If it costs less, the difference is refunded at check-in.
  • To change rooms, cancel and book again, or ask reception.

Your stay

  • Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 11:00. Reception is open 24 hours.
  • Each room has a guest limit: Garden rooms sleep 1, Classic and Executive rooms sleep 2, and Penthouse suites sleep 4. Children count toward the limit.
  • Rooms 106, 206, 306 and 406 are fully accessible. See our accessibility statement.
  • Children under 12 must stay with an adult. Every booking needs at least one adult.
  • Please treat the room with care; damage beyond normal use may be charged.

Promo codes

  • One promo code per booking. Codes are checked when you book and can't be added afterwards.
  • The discount comes off the room price only, not the $30 taxes & fees.
  • Codes have start and end dates and may be withdrawn at any time. Expired codes can't be used.

Privacy

What we collect and why

  • Your account: your name and email, so you can sign in and we can identify your bookings. Your password is stored only as a one-way scrambled "hash" - we can never see it.
  • Your bookings: room, dates, guests, price and status, so we can run your stay.
  • Reviews: if you review a stay, your rating and comments are shown publicly with your name.
  • When you agreed to these terms, recorded when you create an account.

Who can see it

  • Our front-desk staff and manager can see bookings and guest names and emails to run the hotel.
  • We don't sell or share your details for marketing.

Your browser

  • When you sign in, a login token is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. It expires after 8 hours, and signing out removes it. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Outside services

  • Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, styling from the Tailwind CDN, and the contact-page map from OpenStreetMap. These services can see your IP address when they load.
  • Messages you send to Aria, our AI assistant, are sent to an AI provider (Groq, Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude) to write a reply. Aria's conversation is kept in memory only and is cleared when the server restarts.
  • Contact-form messages are delivered to us by email through Web3Forms.

Your choices

  • You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account, by emailing reservations@laextravaganza.co.nz.
