Good to know

Questions guests ask

When can I check in?
Rooms are ready from 3pm and check-out is by 11am. Arriving early? Reception will hold your bags.
Can I cancel my booking?
Yes - cancel free of charge from My Stays until 23:59 the day before you arrive, or move your stay to new dates there. For same-day changes, call reception. See our Terms.
How many people can stay in a room?
Garden rooms sleep one, Classic and Executive rooms two, and Penthouse suites up to four.
Is there a fee on top of the room price?
A flat $30 for taxes and fees is added once per booking - the total is always shown before you pay.
Can you help with a special occasion?
Of course. Tell us in a message above and the concierge will arrange flowers, dinner or a late check-out where we can.
Is there someone I can ask right now?
Aria, our assistant, is in the corner of every page. She can check dates, compare floors and find you a room.
