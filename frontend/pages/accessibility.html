Accessible rooms

There is one fully accessible room on every floor: rooms 106, 206, 306 and 406. Each one has:

  • A roll-in shower with a fold-down seat
  • Grab rails by the toilet and shower
  • A lowered bed, light switches and wardrobe rail
  • 90 cm wide doorways and step-free access by lift
  • A visual and vibrating fire alarm
  • A hearing loop, available on request

To see only these rooms, turn on the Accessible rooms filter on the Rooms page. They're also marked on the floor map.

The building

  • Step-free entrance from Harbour Avenue, with an automatic door.
  • A lift serves every floor, including the courtyard and the restaurant.
  • A hearing loop is fitted at reception.
  • Assistance animals are always welcome, at no extra charge.
  • Accessible parking spaces are next to the entrance; ask reception to reserve one.

This website

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. In particular:

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, and a "Skip to content" link jumps past the menu.
  • Form fields have labels, and errors are explained in words, not colour alone.
  • The floor map can be explored with the arrow keys.
  • Text and controls are designed to meet contrast guidelines, and pages work when zoomed to 200%.

Some parts are still being improved, such as descriptions of every room photo. If something gets in your way, please tell us.
